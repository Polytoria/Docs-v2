# :polytoria-Number: Numbers

The `number` datatype is different from other programing languages such as C# and JavaScript in that it does not separate decimals into a dedicated type. In Luau `0` is the same as `0.0` and even `-0`

Numbers can be anything from -1.7 * 10^308^ to 1.7 * 10^308^ (about 15 digits of precision, positive or negative).

### Notation

Numbers can be in `decimal` such as, `-20`, `0.3` or `-2.4534`. They can also be in `scientific notation` such as `12e5` which is 12 * 10^5^ or 1,200,000. They can also be `hexadecimal` or base-16, `0xf` or 15 for example. Finally, numbers can be in `binary` or base-2. For example, `0b1100` or 12
